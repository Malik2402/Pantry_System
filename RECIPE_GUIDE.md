# Smart Pantry Manager: 40 recipes

This collection includes the original 20 recipes and 20 additions. Suggestions appear only when every required ingredient and quantity is available. Add the ingredient names and units below to try a recipe. Cooking water is listed where required.

## New recipes (21–40)

### Garlic bread

- bread: 2 piece
- butter: 15 g
- garlic: 5 g

1. Finely chop the garlic and mix it into softened butter.
2. Spread over the bread.
3. Toast under a grill until the edges are golden and the butter is bubbling.

### French toast

- bread: 2 piece
- egg: 1 item
- milk: 60 ml
- butter: 10 g

1. Beat the egg with the milk in a shallow dish.
2. Dip both sides of each bread piece into the mixture.
3. Melt the butter in a pan and cook the bread on both sides until golden and the egg coating is fully set.

### Apple cinnamon oats

- oats: 50 g
- milk: 250 ml
- apple: 1 item
- ground cinnamon: 1 g

1. Core and finely chop the apple.
2. Combine all ingredients in a saucepan.
3. Simmer gently for 7 minutes, stirring, until the oats are creamy and the apple has softened.

### Peanut butter oats

- oats: 50 g
- milk: 250 ml
- peanut butter: 20 g

1. Combine the oats and milk in a saucepan.
2. Simmer gently for 5 minutes, stirring until thick.
3. Stir in the peanut butter until evenly mixed and serve.

### Cocoa banana smoothie

- banana: 1 item
- milk: 250 ml
- cocoa powder: 5 g

1. Peel and slice the banana.
2. Blend the banana, milk and cocoa powder until smooth.
3. Pour into a glass and serve.

### Cucumber yoghurt dip

- cucumber: 1 item
- plain yoghurt: 150 g
- lemon juice: 10 ml
- garlic: 3 g

1. Grate the cucumber and squeeze out excess liquid.
2. Finely chop the garlic.
3. Mix the cucumber, yoghurt, lemon juice and garlic, then serve.

### Avocado lemon toast

- bread: 2 piece
- avocado: 1 item
- lemon juice: 10 ml

1. Toast the bread.
2. Remove the avocado skin and stone, then mash the flesh with the lemon juice.
3. Spread over the toast and serve.

### Tomato cheese omelette

- egg: 2 item
- tomato: 1 item
- cheese: 30 g
- olive oil: 5 ml

1. Chop the tomato, grate the cheese and beat the eggs.
2. Warm the oil in a pan and soften the tomato.
3. Pour in the eggs and cook gently until set. Sprinkle over the cheese, fold and let the cheese melt.

### Mushroom spinach pasta

- pasta: 150 g
- water: 1000 ml
- mushroom: 150 g
- spinach: 50 g
- garlic: 5 g
- olive oil: 15 ml

1. Boil the water and cook the pasta following its packet timing, then drain.
2. Slice the mushrooms and finely chop the garlic. Fry the mushrooms in the oil until tender.
3. Add garlic and spinach; stir until the spinach wilts. Toss with the pasta.

### Lemon chickpea rice

- cooked rice: 200 g
- cooked chickpeas: 150 g
- lemon juice: 15 ml
- olive oil: 10 ml
- ground cumin: 2 g

1. Drain the cooked chickpeas.
2. Warm the oil in a pan and stir in the cumin for 30 seconds.
3. Add the chickpeas and cooked rice. Stir until piping hot throughout, then stir in the lemon juice.

### Potato pea curry

- potato: 300 g
- peas: 100 g
- onion: 1 item
- curry powder: 5 g
- olive oil: 15 ml
- water: 300 ml

1. Dice the potatoes and chop the onion. Soften the onion in the oil.
2. Stir in the curry powder, potatoes and water. Cover and simmer until the potatoes are tender, about 20 minutes.
3. Add the peas and simmer for 5 more minutes, uncovering to thicken the sauce.

### Black bean cheese quesadilla

- tortilla: 2 piece
- cooked black beans: 150 g
- cheese: 50 g
- olive oil: 5 ml

1. Drain and lightly mash the cooked black beans; grate the cheese.
2. Spread the beans and cheese over one tortilla and cover with the other.
3. Brush a pan with the oil and cook the quesadilla on both sides until golden and hot through. Cut into wedges.

### Tuna lemon pasta

- pasta: 150 g
- water: 1000 ml
- canned tuna: 100 g
- lemon juice: 15 ml
- olive oil: 10 ml

1. Boil the water and cook the pasta following its packet timing, then drain.
2. Drain the canned tuna and break it into flakes.
3. Toss the pasta with the tuna, lemon juice and olive oil; warm through and serve.

### Cucumber tomato couscous

- instant couscous: 100 g
- water: 150 ml
- cucumber: 1 item
- tomato: 2 item
- lemon juice: 15 ml
- olive oil: 15 ml

1. Bring the water to the boil. Pour it over the instant couscous, cover and leave for 5 minutes until absorbed.
2. Fluff the couscous with a fork and let it cool slightly.
3. Dice the cucumber and tomatoes, then toss everything together with the lemon juice and oil.

### Broccoli cheese baked potato

- potato: 300 g
- broccoli: 100 g
- cheese: 40 g
- water: 250 ml

1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Prick the potatoes and bake until tender, about 45–60 minutes depending on size.
2. Cut the broccoli into small florets. Simmer in the water in a covered saucepan until tender, then drain.
3. Split the potatoes, fill with broccoli and grated cheese, and return to the oven until the cheese melts.

### Tomato butter bean stew

- cooked butter beans: 200 g
- tomato: 3 item
- onion: 1 item
- garlic: 5 g
- olive oil: 15 ml
- water: 150 ml

1. Chop the onion, tomatoes and garlic; drain the cooked butter beans.
2. Soften the onion in the oil, then stir in the garlic for 1 minute.
3. Add tomatoes, beans and water. Simmer gently for 15 minutes until hot through and thickened.

### Honey roasted carrots

- carrot: 300 g
- olive oil: 10 ml
- honey: 10 ml

1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Cut the carrots into thin batons.
2. Toss with the olive oil and spread on a baking tray.
3. Roast for 20 minutes, then toss with honey and roast for 5–10 more minutes until tender.

### Peanut noodles

- noodles: 150 g
- water: 1000 ml
- peanut butter: 30 g
- soy sauce: 15 ml
- lemon juice: 10 ml

1. Boil the water and cook the noodles following their packet timing. Reserve a little of the cooking water, then drain.
2. Mix peanut butter, soy sauce and lemon juice, adding reserved cooking water a spoonful at a time to make a coating sauce.
3. Toss the noodles with the sauce and serve.

### Carrot lentil soup

- carrot: 200 g
- red lentils: 100 g
- onion: 1 item
- ground cumin: 2 g
- olive oil: 10 ml
- water: 600 ml

1. Rinse the lentils and chop the carrot and onion.
2. Soften the onion in the oil, then stir in the cumin.
3. Add carrots, lentils and water. Simmer for 25 minutes until the carrots and lentils are soft. Mash lightly or serve chunky.

### Berry yoghurt bowl

- mixed berries: 100 g
- plain yoghurt: 150 g
- oats: 20 g
- honey: 5 ml

1. Rinse fresh berries, or thaw frozen berries according to their packet instructions.
2. Spoon the yoghurt into a bowl and top with berries and oats.
3. Drizzle with honey and serve.
